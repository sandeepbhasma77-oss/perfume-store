THE HOUSE OF ÉLAN

"Created slowly. Designed to linger."

MAISON PHILOSOPHY

A QUIET APPROACH
TO FRAGRANCE.

ÉLAN was created around a simple belief: fragrance should feel personal. We create compositions that are elegant, complex and quietly unforgettable.

Rather than loud projections that demand attention, our master perfumers practice the art of restraint. Each formula is built around the intimacy of close encounters—subtle warmth, delicate skin scents, and rare botanical distillations that evolve gracefully throughout the day.

Craftsmanship

An uncompromising four-stage formulation discipline.

01

SOURCE

Carefully selected ingredients from sustainable small-crop harvests around the world, honoring natural seasonal potency.

02

COMPOSE

Balanced fragrance structures formulated in Paris by master noses, harmonizing timeless tradition with modern intimacy.

03

REFINE

Every detail considered. Months of patient maceration in dark temperature-controlled cellars to mature nuanced accords.

04

CREATE

A scent made to become yours. Hand-poured into heavy fluted glass flacons with bespoke magnetic closures.

MADE WITH
INTENTION.

Every flacon represents over two hundred hours of formulation, distillation, and artisanal testing.

Our Pillars

The foundational principles guiding every creation.

CRAFT

Pure French haute perfumery methods, refusing artificial haste or synthetic mass reproduction.

QUALITY

Uncompromising grade-one botanical extracts, aged sandalwood oils, and pure floral absolutes.

INDIVIDUALITY

Subtle formulations that react uniquely with your skin chemistry, never masking your essence.

TIMELESSNESS

Enduring olfactory signatures immune to fleeting trends, crafted to be treasured for decades.
